Big picture: five nights, one journey

The book is a dialogue between a skeptical young man and a philosopher over five nights. Each night builds on the last, moving from "my past decides me" to "I can live fully, right now". Click any night on the map to study it.

The core thread

Who is "Adler"?

Alfred Adler — the "third giant" of psychology beside Freud and Jung. His school is called Individual Psychology: the person is an indivisible whole that moves toward goals.

The method

Socratic dialogue: the youth objects, the philosopher answers. Study tip — try to argue the youth's side, then answer it yourself.
